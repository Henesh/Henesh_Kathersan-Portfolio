02 / Final Year Project

SmartSave turns spending into action.

A multi-modal AI financial assistant that lets students log expenses through Telegram, automatically process receipts and receive grounded saving recommendations.

THE PROBLEM

Budgeting is easy to abandon.

Manual expense entry creates friction. Student spending is spread across cash, e-wallets and bank receipts, making consistent tracking difficult.

THE PRODUCT

Make tracking feel effortless.

Send text, a receipt photo or PDF to Telegram. SmartSave routes the input, extracts the data, categorizes it and turns it into useful financial guidance.
