02 · Final Year Project

SmartSave AI Financial Assistant

A privacy-first financial advisory system designed for Malaysian university students — log expenses through Telegram, process receipts automatically, and turn real spending into practical saving advice.

The problem

Students deal with irregular income and informal spending across cash, e-wallets and bank transactions. Traditional budgeting tools often depend on manual tracking or assumptions that do not fit student life.

The solution

SmartSave removes the friction: students can text an expense, photograph a receipt or upload a PDF through Telegram. The system extracts, categorizes, validates and stores the transaction, then generates grounded saving insights.

Multi-modal logging

Free text, photographed receipts and PDF receipts, including support for six Malaysian bank/e-wallet receipt formats.

Intelligent categorization

Weighted keyword classification across eight categories with confidence scoring, contextual merchant disambiguation and amount validation.

Local AI advisory

Mistral 7B runs through Ollama locally. Financial figures are calculated by the application and supplied to the model as grounding context.

Live analytics

React + Recharts dashboard with day/week/month summaries, budget progress, category breakdowns, transactions and AI advice.
